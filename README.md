## 🚀 Featured Projects

- **PTScribe** &nbsp;[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-0E7C61?style=flat-square)](https://ptscribe.kgiacchi.workers.dev/home) <sub>[Source](https://github.com/kyle-giacchi/ptscribe)</sub><br />
  Leading clinical voice transcription apps cost up to $2,000 a year per provider. For the 267,000 practicing physical therapists in the US, a specialty already fighting tight margins and declining reimbursements, that’s a half-billion-dollar tax on the industry just to manage documentation. I built PTScribe to fix this. It’s an open-source transcription tool that brings premium AI scribing to PTs at a fraction of the cost, keeping clinic overhead low so PTs can focus on patient care, not software subscriptions.

- **Ingestion Factory** &nbsp;[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-0E7C61?style=flat-square)](https://ingestion-factory.kgiacchi.workers.dev/)<br />
  Ingestion Factory transforms how teams translate business needs into data strategy by capturing all data details upfront. It replaces fragmented requests with a streamlined workflow that delivers exactly what each team needs:
  * Business Leaders: The specific questions they need answered to move the business forward.
  * Report Creators: The nuance required to ensure they are providing the right answers.
  * Data Architects: The context needed to model the data right the first time.
  * ETL Engineers: The gritty details that make processing never-ending CSV files easier.

- **InstruRental** &nbsp;[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-0E7C61?style=flat-square)](https://instrurental.kgiacchi.workers.dev/)<br />
  InstruRental is a modern solution designed to pull instrument rental and repair shops out of the 1990s. Incumbent software is notorious for clunky UI and trapped data—making basic reporting a nightmare. InstruRental replaces these legacy systems with:
  * Accessible Analytics: Stop fighting your system to find out how many violins you sold YoY. Your data is yours, instantly accessible and easy to read.
  * Modern Workflows: A clean, intuitive interface designed for how modern shops actually handle rentals, repairs, and retail.
  * Built for Growth: No more walled gardens. Just clear insights and streamlined operations to help shop owners focus on the music
